Campus & Location Overview

Situated in the tranquil, scenic landscape of Vahoor in the Raigad district of Maharashtra.

Serene Vahoor Setting

Nestled amidst natural greenery away from urban noise, providing an idyllic and focused learning environment for students.

Regional Accessibility

Conveniently reachable from Vahoor town center and adjacent villages via state transport bus routes and local transit.

Safe & Secure Campus

A disciplined and supportive campus with modern safeguards, purified drinking water, and hygienic facilities.

Transit & Commute

How to Reach Fajandar Junior College

By State Transport (MSRTC Bus)

Regular MSRTC state transport buses connect Vahoor with major bus depots in Raigad district. Student bus concessions (ST passes) are supported.

By Road & Private Transit

Accessible via all-weather district roads with clear signage leading to the college main gate. Ample parking for bicycles and two-wheelers.

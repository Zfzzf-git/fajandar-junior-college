Institutional Guidelines

General Academic Regulations & Examination System

Attendance & Eligibility

  • Minimum 75% attendance required in both theory & practicals.
  • Completion of all term-work, journals, and project files.
  • Conduct and discipline as per Maharashtra State Board norms.

Assessment Scheme

  • Unit Tests and Terminal Examinations in FYJC.
  • Internal Assessment & Practical Board Exams in SYJC.
  • Final HSC Board Examination as conducted by MSBSHSE.

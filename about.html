About Fajandar Junior College

A heritage of academic dedication, social upliftment, and comprehensive secondary education in Raigad.

Our Institution

Fajandar Junior College, Vahoor, is situated in the scenic and culturally rich district of Raigad, Maharashtra. Established to serve the youth of Vahoor and neighboring regions, the college provides comprehensive Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) education across Arts, Commerce, and Science streams.

Our educational framework emphasizes academic sincerity, character formation, moral discipline, and intellectual curiosity. We foster an inclusive atmosphere where every student receives equitable guidance regardless of background or choice of stream.
